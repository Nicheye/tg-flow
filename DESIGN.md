# Design System — TG Flow

## Visual World

TG Flow is a straightforward workspace for reviewing Telegram activity. Its dashboard uses a quiet, light interface that prioritizes useful information and familiar controls.

## Foundations

- **Mode:** Operate.
- **Typography:** system sans-serif for interface and headings; monospace only for code.
- **Palette:** white and light gray surfaces, dark neutral text, and restrained functional colors for errors and status.
- **Surfaces:** thin gray borders and flat white panels, without atmospheric effects.
- **Geometry:** simple 10–12px panel radii, comfortable spacing, and compact controls.

## Interaction

The inbox is a frequent-use tool, so navigation, refresh, reply, archive, and expansion remain immediate. Context appears as a static readable timeline. Reduced-motion users retain clear feedback without position shifts.

## Content Rules

Use clear names for mentions, chats, and conversations. Do not claim AI execution or automation until those capabilities exist, and never fabricate metrics.
